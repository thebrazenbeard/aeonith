Remote access · Read before submitting

You are visiting Aeonith at a Tailscale HTTPS address. If the operator enables public Funnel access, anyone with this address can reach the page, but reviews require an operator-issued bearer token. Do not share your token.

Your browser sends your writing and bearer token over Tailscale HTTPS to the operator-owned Aeonith server. Your writing can then be processed by the operator's local model runner (such as Ollama or KoboldCpp) only if the local model is enabled and configured; an HTTPS connection does not guarantee a working model. Aeonith does not save manuscripts, tokens, or critiques in browser storage, but the server or model may log or retain what you submit. Use this service for private writing only if you trust its operator.
